# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (single page, minimal vanilla JS). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Drivers in Nepean / Ottawa (Ontario, Canada) whose car has been damaged: a minor at-fault accident they would rather settle privately, an insurance claim, rust, a peeling or faded paint job, a scraped bumper. They are stressed, comparing a few local body shops on their phone, and want to know quickly whether this shop is trustworthy, what it will do for them, and how to reach it.

## Product Purpose

MK Auto Body is an independent collision and paint shop at 24 Bentley Ave, Nepean, operating at that location since 1997. The landing page must turn a worried visitor into a phone call or a quote request.

## Positioning

- Independent alternative to the insurer's "preferred" shop: same warranty, deductible covered up to $500, faster turnaround, rental booked and picked up at the shop.
- Private settlement for minor at-fault accidents: repairs both vehicles so the visitor can avoid an insurance claim.
- In-house paint mixing on PPG Envirobase High Performance waterborne basecoat, matched from the car's OEM paint code with PPG software.
- Same address since 1997.

## Operating Context

- Hours: Mon–Fri 9:00am–6:00pm, Sat 9:00am–1:00pm, Sun closed.
- Key drop box for early-morning / after-hours vehicle drop-off ("24-hour drop-off").
- Phone (613) 727-4347 · mkautobody@gmail.com · 24 Bentley Ave, Nepean, ON K2E 6T8.
- Hiring: Auto Body Technician – Collision (2–3 yrs experience, on site).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: collision repair, insurance claims, accident repair, rust repair, bumper repair, colour match, paint peeling repair. All work comes with warranty. High-quality replacement parts, qualified staff.

The contact form has no backend yet; it must degrade to a mailto/phone action until one is wired.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: "MK" wordmark, M in black and K in red, heavy geometric letterforms with diagonal shear cuts, on white. The user made the logo the binding basis of the visual standard.
- Requested feel: premium, minimalist.
- Language: English (Canadian spelling).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real shop photos in `assets/img/`: before/after collages (`g_*`), paint-booth and finished-car photos (`h_959151451`–`h_959151460`), office photo (`h_959151449`), logo (`h_959151447.jpg`). Downloaded from mkautobody.net with the user's approval.
- Stock images from the old site (`t_*`, handshake, stressed driver, insurance form) are not shop evidence; avoid.
- No testimonials, review scores, job counts, or certifications on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the visitor's situation (accident, claim, rust, paint) and the concrete thing MK does about it.
2. Show the work: real before/after photos are the proof, not adjectives.
3. Phone first; every section is one tap from calling.
4. State only facts the shop has stated.
